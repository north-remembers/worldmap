Turkey

Basic Facts About Turkey

  • Capital: Ankara
  • Continent: Mainly Asia, with a smaller part in Europe
  • Currency: Turkish Lira
  • Language: Turkish
  • National Day: 29 October
  • Region: Western Asia and Southeastern Europe

Turkey, officially called Türkiye, is a country located between Europe and Asia. It is known for its rich history, cultural heritage, historic buildings and beautiful landscapes.
